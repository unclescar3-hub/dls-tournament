🔑 How to set up Flutterwave:
  1. Sign up at flutterwave.com and complete KYC verification
  2. Go to Settings → API Keys — copy your Secret Key and Public Key
  3. In Replit: go to Secrets and add FLUTTERWAVE_SECRET_KEY and FLUTTERWAVE_PUBLIC_KEY
  4. The platform will automatically activate Flutterwave multi-currency payments once the keys are set

Active Queue

How It Works

▶ Players join the queue from the public site.
▶ The moment 2 players join, they are instantly matched — no waiting for more.
▶ Both players receive an email + in-app notification with their opponent name and a unique Game Code.
▶ After the match, players submit screenshots through their dashboard. The AI system verifies the result.
▶ Other players can keep joining while existing matches are in progress.
