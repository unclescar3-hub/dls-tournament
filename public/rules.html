TOURNAMENT RULEBOOK

All competitions run under these rules. Ignorance is not an excuse — read carefully.

1. Registration

  • All players must create a verified account with a real email and phone number.
  • Entry fees must be paid via Paystack before the tournament start date. No refunds after registration.
  • One account per player. Duplicate accounts result in permanent ban.
  • You must use the same in-game username as your registered platform username.

2. Match Conduct

  • Players must arrange and play their matches within the deadline set for each round.
  • Failure to play within deadline results in a walkover (0-3 loss) for the absent player.
  • Both players must agree on a match time via the platform or WhatsApp group.
  • Any form of cheating, exploits, or unsportsmanlike behaviour results in immediate disqualification.
  • Lobby settings must use official Game Day Royal Tournaments match configuration as posted in the tournament announcement.

3. Result Submission

  • The winning player must submit the match screenshot within 1 hour of the match ending.
  • Screenshot must clearly show both players' usernames and the final score.
  • Results are automatically verified by our AI. High-confidence results are approved instantly.
  • Disputed or low-confidence results go to admin review within 24 hours.
  • Submitting false or edited screenshots results in a lifetime ban.

4. Standings & Points

  • Win: 3 points | Draw: 1 point each | Loss: 0 points
  • Tiebreakers (in order): Points → Goal Difference → Goals Scored → Head-to-Head
  • For Knockout tournaments: draws go to Sudden Death extra time, then penalties per game rules.
  • Standings update automatically within minutes of a result being approved.

5. Prize Distribution

  • Prize pools are calculated from the total entry fees collected, minus the platform fee (10%).
  • Prizes are distributed via bank transfer or mobile money within 48 hours of tournament completion.
  • Winners must provide a valid Nigerian bank account or mobile wallet to receive payment.
  • Default prize split: 1st = 50%, 2nd = 30%, 3rd = 20% (may vary by tournament).

6. Bans & Disputes

  • All admin decisions are final. Disputes must be raised within 12 hours of a result being published.
  • Contact us via the official WhatsApp group or admin contact with screenshot evidence.
  • Toxic behaviour, harassment, or threats against other players or admins = permanent ban.
  • Banned players forfeit any prizes and are not eligible for refunds.

By registering for any Game Day Royal Tournaments tournament, you agree to all rules above.
